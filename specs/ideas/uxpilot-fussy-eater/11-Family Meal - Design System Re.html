Accessibility Notes for DnD

  • Ensure keyboard navigation allows moving items via Space/Enter to select, Arrow keys to move, and Space/Enter to drop.
  • Provide aria-live regions announcing "Item grabbed", "Moved to Tuesday", and "Dropped".
  • Maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio on all badges and text within cards.
  • Focus states must use focus:ring-brand-mustard for clear visibility.
