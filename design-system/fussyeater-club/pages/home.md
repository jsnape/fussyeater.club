# Home Page Overrides

> PROJECT: FussyEater Club
> PAGE: Home
> PURPOSE: Convert first-time parents into recipe/meal-plan users quickly.

## Overrides

### Layout

- Max width: `1200px` for normal sections.
- Hero content width: `720px` max to keep copy readable.
- Section order: Hero > Trust Strip > Feature Grid > Meal Plan CTA.

### Content Emphasis

- Lead with family outcome, not app features.
- Keep hero headline under 10 words where possible.
- Add one proof point near CTA (for example: recipes filtered for allergies and picky eaters).

### Component Tweaks

- Primary CTA button can be slightly larger on this page (`min-height: 48px`).
- Feature cards should use equal height at desktop, natural height on mobile.

### Motion

- Hero entrance can use staggered reveal (150-250ms).
- Disable non-essential motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Home-Specific Checklist

- [ ] Above-fold CTA visible at 375px wide.
- [ ] No paragraph exceeds ~75 characters per line on desktop.
- [ ] Trust strip appears before scrolling on common laptop sizes.
