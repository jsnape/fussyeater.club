Noodle Nest with Hidden Veggies

Veggie-heavy Quick Dairy-free
PREP TIME 15m
COOK TIME 20m
YIELD 4 servings

restaurant_menu The Method

1

Boil the noodles according to package instructions until just al dente. Drain and rinse under cold water to prevent sticking.

2

Using a spiralizer or a box grater, shred the carrots and zucchini into long, thin strands that mimic the shape of the noodles.

3

Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small bowl until smooth. The coconut aminos provide a gentle sweetness kids love.

4

Toss the noodles, raw veggie strands, and sauce in a large pan over low heat for 2-3 minutes. The veggies should soften slightly but retain their "noodle" texture.

lightbulb

Mom's Tips

"Swap broccoli for cauliflower if they're in a 'white food' phase. The cauliflower blends perfectly with the noodles and is virtually invisible once the sauce is tossed through."

shopping_basket Ingredients

Noodles & Sauce

  • 250g Egg Noodles
  • 2 tbsp Coconut Aminos
  • 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil

The Veggies

  • 2 Large Carrots (Spiralized)
  • 1 Medium Zucchini (Spiralized)
  • 1/2 cup Finely Chopped Broccoli
