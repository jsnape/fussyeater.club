Crunchy Smooth

Crispy Chicken & Buttered Pasta

A reliable favorite that combines the ultimate safe food (buttered pasta) with a homemade, extra-crunchy take on chicken tenders.

Prep Time

15 mins

Cook Time

20 mins

Servings

4

Household Compatibility

Alex

Contains Dairy

Mia

Safe Match

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Chicken Breast, cut into strips

  • 1 cup Panko Breadcrumbs

  • 1/2 cup Safe Milk Alternative

  • 16 oz Favorite Pasta

  • 2 tbsp Butter or Dairy-Free Spread

    Contains Dairy

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Prep the oven and pasta water

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the pasta.

Sensory Note

Keep the kitchen fan on if boiling water sounds or steam are overwhelming.

2

Coat the chicken

Dip each chicken strip into a shallow bowl of safe liquid, then roll tightly in the panko breadcrumbs until fully coated. Place on the prepared baking sheet.

Sensory Note

Ensure the coating is even. Bare spots can feel 'slimy' compared to the crunchy parts.

3

Bake & Boil

Bake the chicken for 15-20 minutes until golden and cooked through. Boil pasta according to package directions, then drain.

4

Serve

Toss warm pasta with butter or safe spread. Serve chicken alongside, keeping components separate on the plate.

Sensory Note

Use a divided plate if foods touching is a concern.
