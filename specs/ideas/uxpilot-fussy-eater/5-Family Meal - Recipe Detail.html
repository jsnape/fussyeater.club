Crispy Chicken & Buttered Pasta

Prep: 15m
Cook: 20m
Serves: 4

A reliable favorite that combines the ultimate safe food (buttered pasta) with a homemade, extra-crunchy take on chicken tenders. Predictable textures and mild flavors make this a stress-free dinner option.

Family Compatibility

Safe for Leo

No Peanuts, No Dairy (using suggested swaps).

Texture Alert: Crunchy

Chicken tenders have a very crunchy exterior. See 'Deconstructed View' for serving tips.

Ingredients

8 Items

  • Chicken Breast

    1 lb, cut into strips

  • Panko Breadcrumbs

    1 cup

  • Butter

    Flagged: Dairy (Leo)

    SWAP APPLIED

    Vegan Butter Spread

    2 tbsp

  • Penne Pasta

    8 oz

Cooking Mode

Instructions

1

Prep the oven and pasta water

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the pasta.

2

Coat the chicken

Dip each chicken strip into a shallow bowl of safe liquid (like unsweetened oat milk or a flax egg if avoiding real eggs), then roll tightly in the panko breadcrumbs until fully coated.

Sensory Tip: Ensure the coating is even. Bare spots can feel "slimy" compared to the crunchy parts, which might cause rejection.

3

Bake the chicken

Place the coated strips on the prepared baking sheet. Spray lightly with olive oil spray for extra crispiness. Bake for 18-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crunchy.

4

Cook and toss the pasta

While chicken bakes, cook the penne according to package directions. Drain well, then return to the warm pot. Toss gently with the vegan butter spread until melted and glossy. Add a tiny pinch of salt if desired.

Plating & Sensory Tips

Keep it Separate

Use a divided plate. Ensure the buttery pasta does not touch the crispy chicken to maintain the dry, crunchy texture of the tenders.

Safe Dips

Offer a familiar, safe dip (like plain ketchup or dairy-free ranch) in a separate small bowl. Do not pour sauce over the chicken.
